The Hidden Cost of Renting
What You're Really Paying When You Rent
Most renters focus on the monthly number. But move-in costs, pet fees, parking premiums, and zero equity make the true cost significantly higher than the sticker price.
✕

Massive Move-In Costs

First month's rent + security deposit + pet fees + admin fees can easily total $3,000–$4,500 before you even get the keys — and none of it builds equity.

✕

Pet Penalties & Breed Restrictions

Pet deposit ($200–$500) + non-refundable pet fee ($200–$400) + pet rent ($15–$30/month per pet). Many complexes ban breeds like Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, Dobermans, and German Shepherds outright.

✕

Parking Is a Luxury

Carport: $30–$50/month. Detached garage: $100–$150/month. Downtown garage: $75–$150/month. No guaranteed guest parking.

✕

Mandatory Add-Ons

Valet trash ($25/month), pest control ($5–$10/month), package lockers ($5–$20/month) — often required, never optional.

✕

Noise & Neighbor Issues

Stomping from above. Bass from below. Shared walls, shared stress. You have zero control over who lives next to you.

✕

No Equity — Ever

Every rent check pays your landlord's mortgage, builds their wealth, and leaves you with nothing.

✕

Rent Increases Are Inevitable

Texas has no rent-control laws. Your landlord can raise rent at every lease renewal.

✕

Early Lease Break Penalties

Need to move? Most Texas leases charge 2 months' rent + a reletting fee — OR you're on the hook for remaining rent until they find a replacement tenant.

$0 Down Is Real
Programs That Eliminate Your Down Payment
San Antonio buyers have access to multiple state and local programs that can cover 100% of down payment and closing costs.

USDA Rural Development Loan

100% financing with no down payment required. Many suburban San Antonio communities in Converse, Schertz, Cibolo, and beyond qualify as USDA-eligible areas.

0% Down Payment

San Antonio HIP 80

The city's Homeownership Incentive Program provides $1,000–$30,000 in assistance as a 0% interest, no-payment second loan. Fully forgivable if you stay in the home 5–10 years.

Up to $30,000 Forgivable

TSAHC & TDHCA Programs

TSAHC and TDHCA state programs offer 3%–5% of the loan amount in grants or forgivable second liens. Combinable with FHA, VA, USDA, or conventional loans.

Up to 5% Grant — No Repayment

Builder Closing Cost Coverage

National builders like Lennar and D.R. Horton routinely cover all closing costs when you use their preferred lender — often saving $8,000–$12,000 at the table.

$0 Closing Costs

VA Loan (Military & Veterans)

Joint Base San Antonio is one of the largest military installations in the U.S. Eligible service members get 0% down VA loans, no PMI, and competitive rates. Disabled veterans also get Texas property tax exemptions.

0% Down / No PMI / Tax Exempt

FHA 3.5% + DPA Stack

Combine an FHA loan (3.5% down) with a state DPA grant of up to 5%. The grant covers the entire down payment, resulting in $0 cash out of pocket.

Stackable to $0 Out-of-Pocket

Common Questions
San Antonio Buy vs. Rent FAQ
The most common questions I get from clients — answered directly so you can move forward with confidence.
Is it cheaper to rent or buy a home in San Antonio in 2026?+
When you factor in equity building, tax advantages, and the Texas homestead exemption, buying is typically a better long-term financial decision than renting in San Antonio. Renters pay $18,000–$22,000 per year with zero equity gained, while buyers build thousands of dollars in equity during year one alone. Use the calculator above to run your own numbers, or contact Emily Russell for a personalized analysis.
What is the minimum down payment to buy a home in San Antonio?+
In San Antonio, you can buy with as little as $0 down through VA loans (military veterans), USDA loans (eligible suburban areas), or by stacking FHA with the San Antonio HIP 80 program or TSAHC grants. FHA requires 3.5% minimum; Conventional starts at 3%.
What is the San Antonio HIP 80 program?+
The San Antonio Homeownership Incentive Program (HIP 80) provides between $1,000 and $30,000 in down payment and closing cost assistance as a 0% interest, no-payment second loan. The loan is fully forgivable if you remain in the home for 5–10 years. Income must be at or below 80% of Area Median Income, and the property must be within San Antonio city limits.
Can military veterans buy a home in Texas with no down payment?+
Yes. Eligible service members, veterans, and surviving spouses can use a VA loan to purchase a home in Texas with 0% down, no private mortgage insurance, and competitive fixed rates. Given that Joint Base San Antonio is one of the largest military installations in the U.S., VA loans are especially common here. Disabled veterans also receive Texas property tax exemptions ranging from $5,000 to full exemption.
What is the Texas VA property tax exemption?+
Texas offers property tax exemptions to disabled veterans on a sliding scale: 10–29% rating gets $5,000 exemption, 30–49% gets $7,500, 50–69% gets $10,000, 70–99% gets $12,000, and 100% disability rating results in full property tax exemption. Veterans aged 65+ with a 10%+ rating get an additional $12,000. Apply with your county appraisal district.
How much are typical move-in costs for a San Antonio apartment?+
Typical San Antonio move-in costs range from $1,700 to $4,500, including first month's rent, security deposit ($150–$500 with good credit or up to one month's rent), admin fee ($100–$250), application fee ($50–$75 per person), and pet fees (pet deposit $200–$500 plus non-refundable pet fee $200–$400 per pet). Compare that to $0 down programs available to buyers.
What happens if I break my apartment lease early in Texas?+
Most Texas leases offer a buyout clause of two months' rent plus a reletting fee ($500–$1,000). Alternatively, tenants may be obligated to continue paying rent until the lease ends or the landlord finds a replacement, whichever comes first. Penalties typically range from $3,000–$10,000+. Homeowners, by contrast, can sell, rent out, or refinance — the home is an asset, not a trap.
